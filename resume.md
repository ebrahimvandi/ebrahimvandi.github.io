# Alireza Ebrahimvandi, PhD

Website: https://ebrahimvandi.com/ · LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/ebrahimvandi/ · ORCID: https://orcid.org/0000-0002-6338-2662 · GitHub: https://github.com/ebrahimvandi

## Summary

Alireza Ebrahimvandi is a data scientist at UCSF Health who specializes in clinical AI. He validates AI and machine-learning models for production use in a health system and monitors their performance after deployment, including Epic's 30-day readmission model, an LLM-based cancer clinical-trial matching tool, and 1-year admission and ED-visit risk models. He builds and evaluates large language model (LLM) summarization of inpatient notes, hospital course, and discharge instructions, and develops real-time bed-availability prediction and patient-flow simulation for hospital operations.

He has more than ten years of experience in healthcare analytics and research. He holds a PhD and an MS in Industrial and Systems Engineering from Virginia Tech, and his peer-reviewed research applies machine learning, system dynamics, and operations research to preterm birth, infant mortality, PTSD, and healthcare-system performance (130+ citations on OpenAlex).

**Areas of expertise:** clinical AI · AI model validation and post-deployment monitoring · LLM evaluation for clinical text · LLM-based clinical summarization · predictive modeling · forecasting · discrete-event simulation · causal inference and quasi-experimental design · operations research
