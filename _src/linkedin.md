## About

I’m a data scientist at UCSF Health, where I build, validate, and monitor clinical AI.

Current work:
• Validating AI models for production use and monitoring their performance after deployment; projects include Epic’s 30-day readmission model, LLM-based cancer clinical-trial matching, 1-year admission and ED-visit risk models, and Inpatient Insights
• Building and validating LLM-based summarization of inpatient notes, hospital course, and discharge instructions
• Developing real-time bed-availability prediction models for the Patient Capacity Management Center
• Building simulation frameworks that optimize patient flow and inform strategic decisions such as MRI capacity expansion

Before UCSF, I was a healthcare data scientist at the University of Maryland Medical System. I built discrete-event simulations that cut emergency department wait times by 2–4 hours, developed COVID-19 hospitalization and ICU forecasts that outperformed external benchmarks, and designed quasi-experimental studies to inform executive leadership on improving patient satisfaction.

I hold a PhD and an MS in Industrial and Systems Engineering from Virginia Tech, where my doctoral research, which included a project funded by the Ohio Department of Medicaid, used system-dynamics and machine-learning models to study infant mortality and preterm birth. My work appears in six peer-reviewed journal articles and a book chapter, from early signs of preterm birth in U.S. birth records (code on GitHub) to a model of PTSD among military personnel and veterans (covered by Newsweek and Business Insider).

I welcome conversations about clinical AI, health-system analytics, research collaborations, and new opportunities.
